# INF Treaty case study

**Day 4 · 1h · Case study → wargame · 🟡 stub**

How a verification regime worked, how it eroded, and what that implies for AI agreements.

## Notes

Worked 1988–2001: short-notice on-site inspections, continuous portal monitoring, full data exchange — 2,692 missiles verified destroyed by 1991.
Article XI sunset the inspection regime automatically after 13 years. Both sides let it lapse in 2001 and fell back on satellites; the Special Verification Commission atrophied, last meeting Dec 2017.
By the political collapse (US withdrawal Feb–Aug 2019 over the 9M729/SSC-8, Russia counter-alleging on Mk-41 launchers in Romania) the monitoring had already been gone 17 years.
