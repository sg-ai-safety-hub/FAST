Blue protocols

ranked by worst-case safety

Every number is safety — the share of episodes that end with no backdoor deployed (1.00 = the attacker never got one through). useful is the other axis: how much of U's value the protocol keeps. worst is the ranking number — safety against the protocol's worst opponent on the board, not an average. The columns after it are that same safety against each individual attacker, so worst is a summary across them, read differently from a single matchup cell.

Red attacks

ranked by safety forced down

Same safety score, read from the attacker's side: lower means more backdoors got deployed, so a strong attack drives it down. worst is the ranking number — the lowest safety this attack forced on any protocol on the board. The columns after it are the safety it forced on each individual protocol, so worst is a summary across them, not one more matchup.
